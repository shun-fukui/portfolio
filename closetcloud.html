ClosetCloud

RENT IT, ROCK IT, RETURN IT!

ClosetCloud aims to promote clothing sustainability by offering people the option to rent clothes for a much cheaper price.

TYPE UX Project
ROLE UX Designer
TEAM 4 People
DURATION Aug 2025 - Nov 2025
TOOLS Figma, Premiere Pro

01 ★ PROBLEM & SOLUTION

Problem

ClosetCloud addresses the problem of overconsumption in the fashion industry, as it causes significant harm to the environment.

Waste

92 million tonnes of textile waste is generated per year worldwide with around 85% of textiles ending up in landfills or are burnt.

Fast Fashion Culture

Trendy clothes and styles are produced quickly in a large volume and sold at a cheap price. This encourages consumers to purchase frequently and dispose.

Our Solution

A peer-to-peer clothing rental platform that connects people who want to rent, lend, or share affordable outfits directly from one another’s closets.

02 ★ IDEATION

Identifying Stakeholders

The diagram outlines the stakeholders involved in this problem domain, grouped into two main categories: users and providers of clothing items.

We found 3 major themes that resulted from opposing goals and priorities within stakeholders, we decided to tackle these themes through our solution.

Early Interviews & Findings

Based on our stakeholder diagram, we interviewed some stakeholders to gain insights as part of our ideation process.

03 ★ FEATURES

Features

Condition Report

Lenders must complete a condition report when lending out an item of clothing, describing the item’s quality. Upon receiving the item, the renter cross-checks with the condition report.

Sizing Compatibility

Users can save their body measurements on their profile, allowing them to use the sizing compatibility feature on different products.

QR Code

Renters receive a unique QR code which can be scanned by the lender when receiving the item. This ensures smooth verification of identity.

Bargain to buy

Users have the option to bargain to buy a clothing item. This allows users to save money from repeatedly renting and further strengthen sustainability.

04 ★ RESEARCH

Task Scenarios

We conducted a usability test on 8 participants using 4 different task scenarios, one for each of our main features. We gathered qualitative data through open-ended questions and quantitative data through a system usability scale with options ranging from strongly agree to strongly disagree.

First Click Test

Highlighted Navigational Ambiguity

Despite the task being the same, the clicks were spread out. Participants displayed confusion due to information overload, which increased cognitive load and decision paralysis.
