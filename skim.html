CV

Find Me - SKIM

PRACTICALITY × ORIGINALITY

Designed an album cover and branding for an amateur artist named SKIM. The project focused on balancing cost and uniqueness for an artist just starting out.

TYPE Graphic Design, Branding
ROLE Designer (Solo Project)
DURATION 6 Weeks
TOOLS Photoshop, Premiere Pro

01 ★ STRATEGY

Context

The client is an amateur artist with limited resources. We needed a high-impact solution that didn't break the bank.

Core Strategy

Develop a marketing strategy using low-cost promotional mediums while maintaining a unique identity.

02 ★ MUSIC VIDEO

Concept

A journey to "find myself", looking back on memories from the past.

Visual Technique

Utilized ratio changes between 16:9 (present) and 4:3 (past) to visually contrast timelines.

03 ★ ALBUM COVER & LOGO

Album Cover Design

The song title "FIND ME" is designed to emerge from both the front and back of the camera lens to avoid revealing the subject's location.

Logo Design

  • Interpretation: Represents SKIM's songs as a journey of searching for oneself through life experiences.
  • Message: During live performances, the fans' eyes should be focused solely on SKIM.

04 ★ MERCHANDISE

ITEM 01

Exclusive Vinyl Records

While costly to produce, making them exclusive to live events adds a sense of exclusivity to attract fans.

ITEM 02

Guitar Picks

Small, inexpensive, and essential for the artist.

Interaction: Artists can throw them into the crowd as a token of appreciation, leveraging the intimacy of amateur performances.
